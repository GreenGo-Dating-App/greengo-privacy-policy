# GreenGo Privacy Policy

Official Privacy Policy documents for GreenGo Chat application.

## Overview

This repository contains the privacy policy for GreenGo Chat, a cross-cultural discovery and social networking application operated by **Universo Kardasha Ltda.**, based in Sao Paulo, Brazil.

## Compliance

The privacy policy is written to meet the requirements of:

- **LGPD** - Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados, Lei n. 13.709/2018)
- **GDPR** - European Union General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation 2016/679)
- **CCPA/CPRA** - California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act
- **PIPEDA** - Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (Canada)
- **Privacy Act 1988** - Australian Privacy Principles
- **POPIA** - Protection of Personal Information Act (South Africa)
- **PDPA** - Personal Data Protection Act (Singapore)
- Other international data protection regulations

## Current and previous version

The files in the repository root are the current version (5.3.2). 5.3.2 applies from October 22, 2026 to accounts created on or after October 16, 2026 (5.3.1 applied to them from October 17, 2026, 5.3.0 on October 16, 2026). Accounts created before October 16, 2026 stay on 5.2.1 until November 15, 2026 (30 days' notice, Terms 24.2); from November 16, 2026 5.3.2 applies to all accounts. 5.3.2: automatic translation of content written by other people only after AI-services consent (2, 9.1, 9.2); scheduled deletion for sellers with unrefunded sales (15.1).

The version that still applies to some accounts lives in `previous/` with the same file names (`previous/privacy-policy-<lang>.txt`), with a note under the header saying to whom and until when it applies. On November 16, 2026 the `previous/` folder is deleted.

An announced version that is not yet in force goes in `upcoming/` (same file names) until its effective date.
