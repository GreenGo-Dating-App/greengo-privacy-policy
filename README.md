# GreenGo Privacy Policy

Official Privacy Policy documents for GreenGo Chat application.

## Overview

This repository contains the privacy policy for GreenGo Chat, a dating application operated by **Universo Kardasha Ltda.**, based in Sao Paulo, Brazil.

## Compliance

The privacy policy is compliant with:

- **LGPD** - Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados, Lei n. 13.709/2018)
- **GDPR** - European Union General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation 2016/679)
- **CCPA/CPRA** - California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act
- **PIPEDA** - Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (Canada)
- **Privacy Act 1988** - Australian Privacy Principles
- **POPIA** - Protection of Personal Information Act (South Africa)
- **PDPA** - Personal Data Protection Act (Singapore)
- Other international data protection regulations
